How SecCut works

Splitting a video takes just a few simple steps.

1

Choose a video

Upload an MP4, MOV, or WEBM video from your computer or mobile device.

2

Select clip length

Choose between 15-second and 30-second clips depending on how you want to divide your video.

3

Download your clips

SecCut processes the video and gives you individual clips that you can download.

Frequently asked questions

Some common questions about SecCut.

What is SecCut?

SecCut is an online video splitting tool designed to divide a video into smaller clips based on a selected duration.

What video formats are supported?

SecCut currently supports MP4, MOV, and WEBM files.

What clip lengths can I choose?

You can currently choose 15-second or 30-second clips.

Can I download individual clips?

Yes. Once processing is complete, each generated clip can be downloaded individually.

Can I download all clips together?

Yes. SecCut provides a ZIP download option so the generated clips can be collected into one file.

Do I need video-editing software?

No. SecCut is designed to handle the basic splitting process without requiring desktop video-editing software.
