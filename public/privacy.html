Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the SecCut website and video-splitting service.

Information you provide

When you use SecCut, you may upload a video file to the service for processing. The uploaded file is used to perform the requested video-splitting operation.

Do not upload confidential, private, or sensitive material unless you are comfortable with the way the service processes uploaded files.

Uploaded videos

SecCut needs access to an uploaded video in order to process it. The technical handling, temporary storage, processing, and availability of generated files depend on the server-side implementation of the service.

Users should avoid uploading files containing information that they do not have permission to process.

Cookies and similar technologies

SecCut may use technologies such as cookies or similar mechanisms where required for website functionality, analytics, advertising, security, or other legitimate purposes.

Advertising

SecCut may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. These services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and applicable settings.

If advertising is enabled on SecCut, advertising providers may collect information according to their respective privacy policies and Google's applicable advertising policies.

Third-party services

SecCut may rely on third-party infrastructure or services to operate the website, process requests, deliver files, provide advertising, or maintain website functionality.

Children

SecCut is not intended to encourage children to provide personal information. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of online services.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service changes or when additional legal or operational requirements apply. The updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
