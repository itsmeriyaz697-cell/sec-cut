Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using SecCut.

What is SecCut?

SecCut is an online tool for splitting videos into smaller clips. Users can upload a supported video and choose a clip duration.

Which video formats are supported?

SecCut currently supports MP4, MOV, and WEBM video files.

Can I split a video into 15-second clips?

Yes. SecCut currently provides a 15-second clip option.

Can I create 30-second clips?

Yes. You can select the 30-second option before starting the video-splitting process.

How do I upload a video?

Click “Choose Video” on the main SecCut page and select a supported video from your device. On supported desktop browsers, you can also drag and drop a video into the upload area.

How long does processing take?

Processing time depends on the size and characteristics of your video as well as current server workload.

Can I download clips separately?

Yes. Generated clips can be downloaded individually from the results section.

Can I download all clips together?

The results interface provides a ZIP download option when generated clips are available.

What should I do if my upload fails?

Check that the file is an MP4, MOV, or WEBM video. You can also refresh the page and try again. Very large files may take longer to upload or process.

Does SecCut replace professional video editing software?

No. SecCut is focused on video splitting. Professional editing applications provide many additional features such as effects, transitions, color correction, audio editing, and timeline editing.
