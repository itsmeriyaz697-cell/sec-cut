About SecCut

SecCut is a simple online video splitting tool designed to make dividing videos into shorter clips easier.

Instead of opening complicated video-editing software for a basic splitting task, SecCut lets users upload a supported video, select a clip duration, and download the resulting clips.

```

What SecCut does

SecCut focuses on one specific task: splitting videos into shorter sections. Users can currently choose between 15-second and 30-second clips.

The tool is useful for people who need to divide longer videos into smaller sections for short-form content, editing, sharing, or other personal projects.

```

Our goal

Our goal is to keep video splitting straightforward. SecCut is designed with a simple interface so that a user can understand what to do without learning a complicated editing application.

Supported videos

SecCut currently accepts common video formats including MP4, MOV, and WEBM. Available processing options may depend on the video and the server configuration.

Built for simplicity

SecCut is intentionally focused rather than trying to become a full video-editing suite. The website provides a dedicated workflow for uploading, splitting, and downloading video clips.
